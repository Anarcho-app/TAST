# Stream 31 — Autosomal Native American ancestry (Bryc et al. 2015)

**is_floor_quantitative = 1**

## Published anchor

Bryc et al., *Am J Hum Genet* 2015, n = 5,269 self-identified African Americans:
mean ancestry ≈ **73.2% African, 24% European, 0.8% Native American**.

## Why floor / H2-relevant

- Not owner/enumerator paperwork.
- Survives `victors_reliability = 0`.
- H2-style material absorption/reclassification of Indigenous populations on American soil, if demographically large, predicts **substantially higher** Native American autosomal ancestry than trace levels.
- Observed ~0.8% is the published figure; it is a **falsification pressure** on strong H2, not a proof of H1.

## Likelihoods (mild, provisional)

H2 = 0.35 (surprising under strong absorption H2)  
H1 = 0.60 (compatible with predominantly African-origin gene flow + minor other)  
H5 = 0.50  

These are **not** a million-to-one ranking. Sample is modern cohort; historical admixture timing is more complex; still a primary-linked genomic observation the floor should not ignore.

# DNA limits (joint model)

O3 uses a Bryc-class mean only as a **cohort admixture summary**.

It does **not**:
- Census ~40M+ African Americans
- Prove 5–7 racial boxes through history
- Bound α_admin (lookerism / schedule classification)

It does constrain a narrow product α_I = α_admin · f_I · d only under explicit assumptions about Indigenous share and retention — and even then only for ancestry retained in the sampled self-ID cohort.

# DNA limits

- Cohort NA mean (~0.8%, n≪40M) is **in-sample** under admixture model M — not a national measurement.
- **Category exit:** lineages that left the AA self-ID category bias the observed mean **down**. Treat 0.8% as a **floor**, not a point estimate of historical α_I. O3 uses one-sided/wide-above likelihood + exit_bias latent.
- **K-cluster / f_I:** “Indigenous share” in the model is **panel-relative** (projection onto component X under panel P), not an ethnohistorical headcount fraction. Do not set f_I from census “Indian” counts as if identical.
- **α_I** = α_admin · f_I · d only product O3 sees. **α_admin** (lookerism) is not genomically identified.
- Spatial aggregation: cohort mean flattens regional structure (e.g. Oklahoma elevation) where reclassification hypotheses would predict signal.

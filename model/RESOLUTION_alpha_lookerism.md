# Resolution: lookerism vs genomic channel (2026-07-24)

## Problem
`implied_na = alpha` treated administrative absorption/reclassification (lookerism)
as if it were identical to Indigenous ancestry retained in a modern self-ID cohort.
Race on schedules is enumerator lookerism + law + interest, not genotype.
The proxy chain is not a straight line.

## Split estimands
| Symbol | Meaning | Identified by |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| α_admin | Share of 1860 *category stock* from classification/absorption | Schedule transitions, trials, legal process — NOT NA% alone |
| α_I | Indigenous-associated ancestry contribution retained in modern self-ID AA cohort | Autosomal NA% (Bryc-class) with SE, dilution, sample selection |

## Rule
O3 constrains α_I (or α × f_I × d), never α_admin by identity.
Low modern NA% bounds the Indigenous-retained-in-sample channel.
Non-Indigenous reclassification is not bounded by O3.

## Publishable claim (narrow)
Indigenous-associated absorption **as retained in ancestry of the modern
self-identified African-American population** is bounded in the low single
digits under stated priors — not “all absorption ≤ ~5%.”

## Diagnostics required
- Prior-only / O1+O2 / full / O3-off
- α prior sensitivity (Beta(1,20) is load-bearing)
- Explicit f_I (Indigenous share of absorbed pool) if α_admin is reported
